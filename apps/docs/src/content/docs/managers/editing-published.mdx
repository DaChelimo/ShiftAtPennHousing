A published week changes forward, on the live calendar, one block at a time.

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>Open the calendar at the week to change.</li>
  <li>Select the block to open the override editor.</li>
  <li>Add a worker, or remove the one on it.</li>
  <li>
    Choose the scope, then confirm.
    <span class="step-note">The worker is notified either way.</span>
  </li>
</Steps>

<Table>

| Scope | What it changes | Pick it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This week only | Only the week you are viewing. | A one-off: illness, an event, a trade. |
| This week onward | This week, and that weekday and time in every later week. | The pattern itself is wrong. |

</Table>

## What you'll see

Removing a worker leaves a vacant seat, claimable in <UI>Open Shifts</UI> and escalated if
the desk would be empty.

## Rules that apply

- **Either scope works on a shift of any age**, past or underway, because an override is a
  correction and not a claim. See [How a shift gets covered](/system/coverage-ladder).
- **An override cannot touch a float-committed seat**, because the floater was promised it.
  Decline or void the float first. See [Floating: overview](/system/floating).
- **The 40-hour hard cap is never bypassable**: it refuses the assignment. The 20-hour
  school-year cap only warns. See [Hours, caps, and attribution](/system/hours-rules).
- **An SM edits their own house only.** HM, BA, RSM, and Administrator override any house,
  on the authority that lets them publish. See [Roles and the duty hierarchy](/system/roles).

## If it goes wrong

**The editor refuses the worker.** The seat is float-committed, or the worker would cross
40 hours.

**An onward change hit weeks you did not mean to.** Run a second onward override where the
old pattern resumes.
