# How do I give my shift to someone without a trade

Handing off means giving your hours to another worker without getting hours back. Open My Shifts, tap the shift, tap Swap it, then tap "Choose who to swap with". On the Propose a swap screen, tap the Hand off pill at the top. Choose who should get the hours, then tap Hand off shift. They receive an offer they can accept or decline.

# Choosing who receives the hand off

When you switch to Hand off, the screen shows a recipient directory instead of the swap calendar. You can hand off to eligible workers in your own house or in other houses. Use the search box to find someone by name. If there is nobody eligible you see "No eligible workers in other houses."

# What a hand off looks like to the person receiving it

The person you hand off to sees a single panel, not a two sided swap. It reads "{your name} wants to give you these hours" with a label of "Hours offered" for a one time hand off or "Permanent hours" if it repeats every week. They accept or decline it like any other request. Nothing changes on your schedule until they accept.

# Hand off versus swap

A hand off is one directional: you give hours and get nothing back. A swap is a trade where you also receive hours. If you want a trade, use the Swap pill instead. Both start from the same Propose a swap screen reached through Manage shift.
