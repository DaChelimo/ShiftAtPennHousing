# Packages and mail

Log every delivered package into the package system before it goes on the shelf. Scan the
resident barcode, place the package on the numbered shelf, and the resident receives an
automatic pickup notice. Never hand a package to anyone other than the named recipient
without a photo ID that matches.

# Resident lockout

A resident locked out of their own room should be verified by university ID against the
roster. Once verified, issue a temporary access per the house key procedure and log the
lockout. If the resident cannot be verified, do not grant access; direct them to the house
office during business hours or escalate after hours.

# Elevator out of service

If an elevator stops working, place the out of service sign, log the time, and report it to
facilities through the maintenance line. Note whether anyone is trapped inside; if a person
is trapped, treat it as an emergency and call the emergency line immediately.

# Water leak

For an active water leak, note the location and whether it is spreading. Shut off the local
valve only if you have been trained and it is safe. Report it to facilities and, if it
threatens resident belongings or electrical fixtures, escalate right away.

# Fire alarm

If a fire alarm sounds, follow the building evacuation protocol and call the emergency line.
Do not silence or reset an alarm yourself. A single smoke detector fault affecting one room
is still handled as a life safety event until confirmed otherwise by the fire panel and
responders. The desk assistant does not replace emergency services.
