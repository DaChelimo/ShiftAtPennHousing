# How do I drop a shift

Go to the My Shifts tab and tap the shift you want to drop. This opens the Manage shift sheet. Tap the card that says Drop the shift (its subtitle reads "Opens for others to claim"). Choose how much to drop, then tap the red confirm button at the bottom. Once you confirm, the shift opens for other workers to claim.

# Dropping the whole shift or just part of it

In the Manage shift sheet, if your shift is longer than one block you can choose how much to drop under the "How much" selector. Drag the range, or tap "From now" to drop from the current time to the end. The confirm button updates to match: "Drop this week" drops the whole shift, and "Drop 17:30 - 19:00" drops just the range you picked.

# Dropping one week versus every week

When a shift repeats, the Manage shift sheet shows a choice between "This week only" and "Permanent". Pick "This week only" to drop just this week's shift. Pick "Permanent" to give up that weekly slot for the rest of the period; the confirm button then reads "Drop permanently".

# Dropping a shift that starts very soon

If the shift starts within 20 minutes you see a warning titled "Starts within 20 minutes" that reads "Short-notice drop. Your manager is notified immediately to arrange cover." You can still drop it, but your manager is alerted right away so the desk stays covered.

# What happens after I drop a shift

A dropped shift is released to the open shifts pool so a housemate can claim it. It leaves your My Shifts calendar. Your weekly hours go down by the hours you dropped. If nobody claims it, coverage is handled through your house's normal escalation, so dropping does not leave the desk empty.
