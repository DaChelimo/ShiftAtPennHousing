Your hours are the shifts you hold in one week, counted against that week's cap.

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>Open <UI>My Shifts</UI>.</li>
  <li>
    Read the clock chip under the week header.
    <span class="step-note">
      It reads like <em>This week, 14h of 20h soft cap</em>.
    </span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Tap the header arrows to check another week.
    <span class="step-note">The cap follows the week shown.</span>
  </li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

The total counts every shift you hold that starts in that week.
[Drop one](/workers/dropping) and it falls.

## Rules that apply

- **A week runs Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59.** Each week settles on its own, so nothing
  rolls over. See [Hours, caps, and attribution](/system/hours-rules).
- **Your hours count at your home house wherever you work.** That is definitional, not a
  rule with a reason. A [float](/workers/floating) or a
  [cross-house pickup](/workers/picking-up) changes the desk, not the total.
- **Only scheduled time counts.** Stay late for a floater and those extra minutes are
  lost, because only assigned blocks count.
- **The cap is not always 20 hours.** Winter break, and any week touching Thanksgiving,
  fall, or spring break, runs at 40 hard. A summer season sets its own.

## If it goes wrong

**A warning when you claim.** The week is on a soft cap. Tap through and the shift is
yours.

**The claim is refused.** The week is on a hard cap. No manager can waive it. Drop a shift
that week, or claim in another.

**A permanent pickup skipped weeks.** Any over-cap week is skipped, soft or hard, because
one tap commits you to many weeks.
