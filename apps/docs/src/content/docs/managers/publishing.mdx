title: 'Publishing a schedule'
description: 'What publishing does to a built week: it goes live, every assigned worker is notified, and the period cannot be unpublished.'

Publishing turns your drafted template week into live shifts for the whole period.

<Callout label="There is no unpublish">
  A published period cannot be withdrawn. From then on a week changes only forward, on the
  live calendar.

  Confirming also notifies every worker you assigned, and that notification cannot be
  recalled. At a full house, dozens of phones buzz within the minute.

  The confirmation dialog is the last reversible moment. Read the house name on it first.
</Callout>

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>Check the house switcher and the template week in the builder.</li>
  <li>Select <UI>Publish</UI>.</li>
  <li>Read the dialog, then confirm.</li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

Your template week stamps across every week of the period.
Seats you left empty stay open for claiming, which is how later hires get hours.

## Rules that apply

- **An SM publishes their own house only.** HM, BA, RSM, and Administrator publish any
  house, on the same cross-house schedule authority that lets them override it. See
  [Roles and the duty hierarchy](/system/roles).
- **Nobody can silence the "Schedule published" notification**, because a worker who misses
  it does not know they are on the desk. See
  [What triggers a notification](/system/notifications-rules).
- **The pattern repeats by weekday and time, not by date.** One drafted Tuesday fills every
  Tuesday left in the period, so a clock change never moves a 9:00 start. See
  [The operating calendar](/system/calendar).

## If it goes wrong

**Publish was refused.** You are an SM viewing another house. Switch back, or ask that
house's SM or HM.

**You published the wrong pattern.** Fix it forward with **This week onward**. See
[Editing a published week](/managers/editing-published).
