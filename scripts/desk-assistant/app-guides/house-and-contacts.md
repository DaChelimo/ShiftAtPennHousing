# How do I see who is on the desk

Open the House tab to see the live desk schedule for the week as a grid. The legend shows what the colors mean: "You" is your own shift, "Float-in" is a worker floated in from another house, and "Open" (a dashed cell) is an unfilled seat. Swipe to move between days and weeks.

# Looking at another house's schedule

At the top of the House tab, tap the house header (the chevron labelled "Change house") to open the house switcher and view another house's schedule. Your own house carries a "Your house" badge. Each house row shows its desk phone, or "No desk phone" if none is on file.

# How do I call the desk or a coworker

Tap a worker's cell in the grid to open a contact sheet with their name. Tap "Call {worker}" to dial that person, or "Call the desk" to dial the house desk phone. If no number is on file it shows "No phone on file". These buttons open your phone's dialer; they do not place the call automatically, so you confirm before it dials.
