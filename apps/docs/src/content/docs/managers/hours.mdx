The hours report shows where a worker's week went. The weekly cap is what stops it growing.

<Callout label="A cap change lands on all 13 houses">
  One cap covers a calendar week campus-wide. Setting it for your house sets it for every
  house, because per-house values would enforce unevenly across a campus workers move around.

  It applies the moment you save, with no approval step and no per-house exception.

  Only an HM (Housing Manager) or BA (Building Administrator) may set it. An SM (Student
  Manager) cannot.
</Callout>

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>
    Open <UI>Hours report</UI> and read the week against the cap on the header.
    <span class="step-note">Each worker splits three ways: worked at home, worked while floated out, worked on a cross-house pickup.</span>
  </li>
  <li>If that week's cap is wrong, open <UI>Weekly hours cap</UI>.</li>
  <li>Enter the Monday of the week under <UI>Week beginning Monday</UI>.</li>
  <li>
    Choose <UI>20 hours</UI> soft or <UI>40 hours</UI> hard.
    <span class="step-note">Soft warns the worker and lets them carry on. Hard refuses the claim, and no manager can override it.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Write an audit note, then save.
    <span class="step-note">Stored with your name and the time you saved it.</span>
  </li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

That week's row changes from <UI>Profile default</UI> to <UI>Manual override</UI>, showing
the new number and its soft or hard tag. Every hours meter on both apps reads the new value.

## Rules that apply

- **A week runs Monday to Sunday**, never a rolling seven days, so each week settles alone.
  See [Hours, caps, and attribution](/system/hours-rules).
- **Hours count at the worker's home house**, whatever desk they stood at, so somebody you
  floated out still appears on your report.
- **The report counts assigned time, not worked time.** A worker who stays late waiting on a
  late floater is not credited for those minutes.
- **A float never consults a cap**, because it relocates hours the worker was already
  scheduled to work. See [Floating: overview](/system/floating).
- **A season sets its own cap.** Summer weeks carry whatever the Administrator configured, so
  never assume the number is 20 or 40. See [Operating seasons and calendar](/managers/seasons).

## If it goes wrong

**You lowered the cap and nobody lost hours.** That is correct. Shifts already assigned stand
and pending floats are honored. Only new claims, swaps and floats are refused.

**The house switcher does not move the report.** It follows the switcher for an Administrator
and the HMOD on duty. Everyone else reads their own house.

**The cap screen says managers only.** You are an SM. Ask your HM or BA to make the change.
