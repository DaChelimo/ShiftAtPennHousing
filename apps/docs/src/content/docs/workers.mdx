Your whole relationship with this app is five verbs: **see**, **pick up**, **drop**,
**swap**, and **acknowledge**. Everything else on these pages is context for one of those
five. If you are new, read the first four pages in order on your first day. If you landed
here from a notification, jump straight to the page you need.
