## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>
    Open the app and type your email and password.
    <span class="step-note">Your manager creates your account. You cannot sign yourself up.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Tap <UI>Sign in</UI>.
    <span class="step-note">No PennKey redirect. Cancel a slow attempt and your typing stays.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Read the card about notifications and say yes.
    <span class="step-note">This card is the app asking, not your phone.</span>
  </li>
  <li>Allow notifications in the dialog your phone then shows.</li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

A short tour of the bottom tabs and the Assistant, once.

Then <UI>My Shifts</UI>, your home screen. Settings replays any tour.

## Rules that apply

- **Some notifications have no off switch.** Floats, swap requests, and a published
  schedule need your answer, so they reach you either way. See
  [What triggers a notification](/system/notifications-rules).
- **Saying no on the app's card costs nothing.** It never touches your phone's
  permission, so you can say yes later. See
  [Notifications and widgets](/workers/notifications).
- **The browser has no password field.** A 6 digit code arrives by email. It is a code, not a
  link, because a link tapped on your phone would sign in the phone, not the desk. See [On the web](/workers/web-portal).

## If it goes wrong

**Incorrect email or password.** An unknown address returns the same message, so it never says
which is wrong. Ask your manager.

**Network error, or signing in took too long.** The app gives up after 15 seconds, so your
password was fine. Check your connection.

**Your house is not on the system yet.** Houses go live one at a time, so nothing is
broken. Your manager knows the date.
