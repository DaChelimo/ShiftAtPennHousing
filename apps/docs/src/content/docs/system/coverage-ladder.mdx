The coverage ladder is how the system covers a block nobody is scheduled to work.

No screen shows this ladder.

It runs only when the desk would otherwise be empty. A block with one worker present is
covered, so its empty seats are never broadcast, floated, or bought.

One pass secures at most 4 hours. Later blocks climb the ladder on their own, so a long
empty window is never bought at once.

An Allied request pages the house's Residential Services Manager, then its Housing
Manager, then the Housing Manager on duty, an hour per rung. Nobody else is ever paged.

## Why it works this way

The 2-hour lock is one way. Once an empty desk reaches it, its seats stay locked even
after a floater or Allied arrives. Nobody should pile onto coverage the house already
pays for.

Emptiness is judged when the step fires, so a desk that gains a worker first is never
locked at all.

## What this means for you

At a staffed desk, an open seat stays claimable until the block starts. At an empty one,
your claim must finish before the 2-hour mark. The system decides which, not your clock.
