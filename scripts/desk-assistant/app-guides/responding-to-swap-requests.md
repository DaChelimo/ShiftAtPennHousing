# Where do I see swap requests sent to me

Open the Swaps tab at the bottom. It has three sub tabs with live counts: All, Incoming, and Outgoing. Incoming holds requests waiting on you. Outgoing holds requests you have sent that are waiting on someone else. Requests that need your response also show up in Updates and on the affected shift in My Shifts.

# How do I accept or decline a swap

On an incoming request card, tap Accept to agree to the swap or Decline to turn it down. The card shows "You give" on one side and "You get" on the other so you can see the trade before deciding. For a hand off the "You get" side reads "Nothing back", because you are only receiving hours. The sub label "Needs your response" marks requests waiting on you.

# Cancelling a request you sent

Open the Swaps tab and go to Outgoing. On a request you sent, tap Cancel to withdraw it before the other person responds. While it is still open the card shows how long is left, for example "Expires in 5h". Once it expires it shows "Expired" and no longer needs anyone's action.

# How long do I have to respond

Each request has a deadline shown on the card, such as "Expires in 5h". If you do not respond in time the request expires and the shift stays as it was. Respond before the deadline if you want to accept.
