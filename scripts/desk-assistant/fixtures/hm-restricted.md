# Manager escalation notes

These notes are for housing managers and building managers only. When a situation involves a
conduct concern, a police or fire response, or media contact, the manager on duty handles it
directly and records it in the manager log. Do not delegate these to the desk. Keep the
resident directory and any incident specifics out of desk facing answers.
