# What is a float assignment

A float is when you are asked to cover a desk at another house that is short staffed. When you are picked, a float request appears on My Shifts under a "FLOAT REQUEST" card that reads "You're needed at {house}". You respond by tapping Accept or Decline on the card. Float requests also appear in your Updates feed.

# Does a float change my total hours

No. A float moves an already scheduled shift to another desk; it never adds hours. The app says this on the request: "Your weekly hours don't change." You work the same number of hours, just at a different house for that window.

# Accepting or declining a float

Tap Accept on the float card to confirm you will cover it, or Decline if you cannot. Opening the full float screen shows the desk, when it starts, and a countdown; there the confirm button is labelled Acknowledge (with Decline next to it). Once you acknowledge, it reads "You're covering {house}" and is confirmed. If you decline, the app finds another floater and you see "No problem".

# What if I miss the deadline

Each float has a response deadline with a countdown. If the window passes before you respond, the card shows "The window to respond has passed" and the float is reassigned to another worker. Respond before the countdown runs out if you want to take it.

# Where do float requests show up

Float requests appear in three places: the float card on My Shifts, the Updates feed under More, and a full screen that opens when you tap a float notification. Recent floats you have accepted, declined, or that expired are listed under "Recent float requests" for the past 24 hours.
