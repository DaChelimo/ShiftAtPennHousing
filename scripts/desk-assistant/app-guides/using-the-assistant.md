# What is Snoopy for

Snoopy is the app's desk assistant. It answers desk questions and how to questions about the app. Ask it things like how to claim a shift, how to request a break, who is on duty right now, or what your next shift is. Its answers are grounded in the official documentation and the current duty schedule, and it shows where each answer came from, so it will not make up a procedure.

# How do I open Snoopy

Tap the More tab at the bottom, then tap Ask Snoopy. On the My Shifts screen you can also tap the Ask button to open it directly. The empty screen says "Ask a desk question" and offers a few starter questions you can tap, such as "Who's on duty right now?", "What's my next shift?", "How do I check if a resident has access to a specific room?", and "A resident is locked out of their room. What are the steps?".

# What kinds of questions work best

Ask specific, task focused questions in plain language: "How do I swap a shift?", "Where do I pick up open shifts?", "How do I drop a shift that starts soon?". For questions about who to contact or who is on duty, Snoopy checks the live duty schedule rather than the documentation. If it cannot find a grounded answer it tells you and points you to the right person instead of guessing.

# When to use Snoopy versus asking a manager

Use Snoopy first for how to and where is it questions about using the app, and for quick desk questions covered by the documentation. It is there so you do not have to page a manager for routine questions. For anything urgent, or a real emergency, follow your house's escalation and call the desk or emergency line; Snoopy does not replace emergency services.
