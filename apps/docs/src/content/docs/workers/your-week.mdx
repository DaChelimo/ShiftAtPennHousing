My Shifts is your week in three groups: picked up, dropped, and scheduled, top to bottom.

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>
    Open <UI>My Shifts</UI>, the first tab.
  </li>
  <li>
    Check the week navigator. It sets which week every group below shows.
    <span class="step-note">
      A shift you picked up for next week files there, not here.
    </span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Tap a shift to hand it off.
    <span class="step-note">
      One sheet holds both <a href="/workers/dropping">dropping</a> and{' '}
      <a href="/workers/swapping">swapping</a>.
    </span>
  </li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

The hours total covers the week shown, against that week's cap. Caps are set per week, so
the number moves as you navigate.

### Marks on a card

<Table>

| Mark          | What it means                               |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Small circle  | You claimed this seat yourself.             |
| A house name  | That house's desk, not your own.            |
| Golden border | A break shift, outside your normal pattern. |

</Table>

<StateList>
  <StateCard state="float-out" label="Light purple">
    Another desk for this stretch. Same hours, different house.
  </StateCard>
  <StateCard state="pending" label="(Pending)">
    A float you have not answered. Answer by 10 minutes before it starts.
  </StateCard>
</StateList>

## Rules that apply

- **Hours at another desk still count at your home house**, so this one total is your whole
  week. See [Hours, caps, and attribution](/system/hours-rules).
- **The screen updates itself.** The calendar is the source of truth, so a swap or float
  lands without a refresh.

## If it goes wrong

**A shift is missing.** Check the week navigator first.

**A shift you dropped is gone.** Somebody claimed it, or Allied covered it, and a seat has
one owner. Claim one back only while it is still open.
See [Core concepts](/system/concepts).

**A card names a house that is not yours.** You picked that shift up there, or you were
[floated](/workers/floating).
