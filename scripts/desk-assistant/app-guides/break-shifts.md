# How do I sign up for break shifts

Break coverage (winter break, spring break, and similar) is claimed on its own calendar. Open More, then tap Break shifts. When a break is open you also see a banner titled "{break name} is open" with "Pick your break shifts" that takes you straight there. Break shifts are first come, first served.

# Claiming hours on the break calendar

On the Break shifts calendar, drag across the hours you want to claim. The info card explains it: "First-come, first-served, drag to pick your hours, 40h hard cap". A meter labelled "This break" tracks your total. Review the selection, then tap Claim. A shift you hold shows "You"; one taken by someone else shows "Taken" or their first name. You see "Break shift claimed" when it works.

# Dropping break coverage you claimed

To give back break hours you claimed, select your own coverage on the break calendar and tap Drop. You see "Break shift dropped". Anything you release goes back into the pool for other workers.

# If you have no hours over the break

If you are not working the break at all, turn on the toggle "I have no hours this break" on the Break shifts screen. To claim shifts later, untick it first. If you leave it on you will not be assigned break hours.

# When break sign up opens and closes

Break sign up opens 14 days before the break. While it is open you can drag to claim. Once claiming closes, the info card reads "Claiming closed. Leftover shifts are in Open Shifts", so any remaining break shifts move into the Open tab where they can still be claimed.
