A float moves part of your shift to a desk that would be empty, then returns you.

## How it works

The automated lookup runs two hours before a block that would leave a desk empty. A
manager can also float a worker out by hand, naming the range and destination.

<Table>

|                     | Automated float                      | Manager float               |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| Can you decline it? | Yes, until the deadline              | No. It is a directive       |
| If you never answer | Voided; the desk buys cover          | It stands. You are expected |

</Table>

Acknowledge by 10 minutes before the float starts; the destination is waiting on an
answer.

## Why it works this way

An empty desk means paying for outside cover, and borrowing from a covered desk is
cheaper. Three guards:

- **A source desk never drops below one worker.** A single staffed house lends nobody.
- **Harnwell is never a destination.** It needs training only Harnwell workers hold.
  Harnwell can still send people out.
- **Nothing automated takes a float back.** The destination is owed the cover once
  promised. Only a manager can undo one.

## What this means for you

Your hours do not change. A float relocates hours you were already going to work, so no
weekly cap is consulted.
