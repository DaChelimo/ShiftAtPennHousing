The schedule builder turns submitted preferences into one drafted template week.

As an SM you build your own house only. HM, BA, RSM, and Administrator build any house.

<Callout label="You are drafting one week, not a period">
  The grid holds one template week, so the Tuesday you draft becomes every Tuesday in the
  period.
</Callout>

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>
    Open <UI>Schedule builder</UI> after the submission deadline.
    <span class="step-note">Preferences freeze then, so nothing moves under you.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Drag a span down a day column, one to six hours.
  </li>
  <li>
    Pick a name from the <UI>Preferences</UI> card.
    <span class="step-note">Each name shows its span status and hours remaining against target.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Switch to <UI>Manual</UI> for anyone the card will not let you pick.
  </li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

The tag reads **Draft** until you publish, so nothing here reaches a worker or coverage.

Seats you leave empty open for claiming at publish, which is how later hires get hours.

## Rules that apply

- **Blocked is a hard stop in <UI>Preferences</UI>, advisory in <UI>Manual</UI>,** which
  makes you confirm the override.
- **A worker who submitted nothing is blocked too,** because the system will not hand you
  time they never affirmed.
- **The school year's 20 hour cap is soft,** so going over target only warns you. Forty
  hours is a hard ceiling and refuses.
  See [Hours, caps, and attribution](/system/hours-rules).

## If it goes wrong

**A name is missing from the card.** They submitted nothing. Use the <UI>Manual</UI>
roster.

**A name is there but will not click.** They marked cannot, or left a block unmarked, in
your span.

**The save was refused.** You are an SM on another house. Ask its SM.
