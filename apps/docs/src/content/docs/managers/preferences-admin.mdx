Preferences are the input you build from, so the deadline is what decides who you can build with.

<Callout label="A worker with nothing on record drops out of Phase 1">
  At the deadline, anyone who neither submitted nor pressed no hours is treated as no
  preference on record. They vanish from the preference card and appear only in the full
  Phase 2 roster, so they go unscheduled unless you place them by hand.

  You can override the deadline for them. Entering preferences on somebody's behalf works
  after the window has closed, because a closed window is a worker problem, not yours.
</Callout>

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>
    Open <UI>Preferences oversight</UI> and check the house and period on the header.
    <span class="step-note">An SM (Student Manager) sees their own house. An HM, BA or RSM may inspect any house.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Set <UI>New deadline</UI> and select <UI>Set deadline</UI>.
    <span class="step-note">It must fall on or before the period's start date, and submission closes at end of day in New York.</span>
  </li>
  <li>Watch the roster for who is still marked <UI>Not yet</UI>.</li>
  <li>
    Open a worker's row to paint their week on their behalf.
    <span class="step-note">Same grid the worker uses: preferred, available, cannot, plus their target hours.</span>
  </li>
  <li>Save. Repeat for the next worker.</li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

The counts across the top move as workers respond: submitted, no hours, and not yet.
Reminders go out 5, 3 and 1 days before the deadline to anyone who has not answered.

An on-behalf save writes only that one worker. It never touches anybody else's week, and it
does not reopen submission for the rest of the house.

## Rules that apply

- **Workers cannot change preferences after the deadline**, because you build against a
  fixed input. Only a manager may write past it.
- **You build after the deadline passes.** Moving the deadline later moves the day you can
  start. See [Building a schedule](/managers/building-a-schedule).
- **Blocks a worker left unmarked count as cannot** in Phase 1, so a half-filled grid hides
  somebody from the card as effectively as a refusal.
- **A summer season carries one deadline for all houses**, authored by the Administrator on
  the season. You may still adjust your own. See [Operating seasons and calendar](/managers/seasons).
- **A transferring worker paints their new house.** Preference collection uses the house they
  will belong to at the period start. See [People: hiring, roles, transfers](/managers/people).

## If it goes wrong

**"The preference deadline must fall on or before the period start date."** Pick an earlier
date. There is no collecting preferences for a period already underway.

**The deadline card is locked.** The period is published, so its window cannot move.
Change the live schedule instead. See [Editing a published week](/managers/editing-published).

**"You can only edit preferences for workers in a house you manage."** You are an SM opening
another house's worker. Ask that house's SM or its HM.
