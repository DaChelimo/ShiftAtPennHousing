# How do I pick up an open shift

Open the Open tab (its screen is titled Open Shifts). It has two sub tabs, My House and Others. My House lists open shifts in your own house; Others lists open shifts at houses where you are eligible to pick up. Tap a shift to open the claim sheet, then tap Claim shift. It moves into My Shifts and you see "Claimed. It's now in My Shifts."

# Claiming only part of a shift

In the claim sheet, if a shift is longer than one block, the "How much can you cover?" selector lets you claim just a portion. Drag the range to pick your hours. A meter labelled "This brings your week to" shows your new weekly total against your cap. The confirm button reads "Claim {duration}" for a partial claim.

# What the hours cap warning means

If claiming would put you over your soft cap, a warning appears: "Puts you over the 20h soft cap" with the note "Allowed this period, but your manager sees the overage." You can still claim; the button becomes "Claim anyway". If it would break the hard cap you see "Over the 40h limit, can't claim" and must drop another shift first.

# Picking up a shift permanently

Some open shifts can be taken every week. In the claim sheet these show "Pick up permanently" and a note like "Recurring, {day}, {time}" that repeats weekly. When the app checks the weeks it can give you, it shows "Picking up N of M weeks" and how many are skipped because of conflicts or caps. Confirm with "Confirm pickup" for the whole slot or "Pick up {duration}" for part of it.

# Why do I see no open shifts

On My House, an empty feed reads "No open shifts in your house this week." On Others it reads "No open shifts at houses you can pick up at right now. Common during winter break." That just means nothing is currently open to claim; check another week using the week navigator at the bottom.
