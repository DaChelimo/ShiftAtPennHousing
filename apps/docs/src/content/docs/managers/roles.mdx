Five manager roles run this console with different powers.

<Callout label="Cross-house means the schedule, nothing else">

  anywhere is a campus problem. Hiring, transfers and leave stay own-house for HM, BA and
  RSM, because who works for whom is a house decision.
</Callout>

## The duty on top

HMOD duty rotates weekly among HMs and BAs only. You then hold HM powers at all 13
houses, because outside weekday working hours no other manager is on duty. Coverage pages
end with you.

## If the app refuses you

**Publish or override refused.** You are an SM viewing another house. Switch back, or ask its manager.

**Hire or transfer refused.** Ask that house's manager, or an Administrator.
