Managers do two jobs that have nothing in common. One is planning: turning preferences into
a staffed week, weeks before anyone works it. The other is responding: a desk is about to be
empty in ninety minutes and somebody has to decide what happens. The console puts both in
one place, so this section separates them and says which role does which.

Read **Which manager are you?** first. SM, RSM, HM, BM, and Admin have genuinely different
powers, and following another role's instructions produces a permission error that reads as
a broken app.
