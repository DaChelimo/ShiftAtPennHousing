The desk assistant answers desk questions out of Housing's own documents, in one line you can
say out loud.

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>
    Open <UI>More</UI>, then the desk assistant.
  </li>
  <li>
    Type the question the way you would say it.
    <span class="step-note">"Can a resident of another house sign out a cart?"</span>
  </li>
  <li>Read the first line. That is your answer.</li>
  <li>Open the sources underneath if you need the document itself.</li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

One or two short sentences, then nothing. It does not repeat your question or list what it
looked at, because a resident is standing at your desk waiting.

The documents it used sit under the answer, folded up until you tap them.

## Rules that apply

- **It answers from Housing's documents, not from itself.** No document covers your question
  and it says so, then points you at a person.
- **It knows what time it is.** Who you call for the same problem changes between business
  hours and after hours, so it answers for right now unless you name another time.
- **It can see your schedule, and only yours.** Ask for your next shift or your hours this
  week. It cannot look up anyone else. See [Your hours](/workers/hours).
- **It never touches your shifts.** It cannot claim, drop, swap, float, or cancel anything,
  so every one of those stays a thing you do yourself on the app's own screens.
- **It writes a page, you send it.** When something needs a manager, it drafts the message
  and works out who gets it. Nothing goes out until you read it and tap send. See
  [Roles and the duty hierarchy](/system/roles).

## If it goes wrong

**Fire, injury, or a door emergency.** It hands you the protocol and tells you to call. Call.
It is never the emergency line.

**Someone wants in and it is not sure.** It tells you not to let them in, and who to ask.
Nobody is authorised by an app.

**"I could not find that."** Nothing in the documents answers you. Ask your manager rather
than guessing, because a made-up answer at the desk is worse than no answer.

**You asked what happened to a specific resident.** It will not say. It gives guidance, never
case detail.
