## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>
    Open <UI>Open Shifts</UI>.
    <span class="step-note">
      <UI>Others</UI> lists every house you may work.
    </span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Tap a card under <UI>Weekly open shifts</UI> for one week.
    <span class="step-note">
      <UI>Permanent openings</UI> takes the slot every week left in the period.
    </span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Set <UI>How much can you cover?</UI> to take part of it.
  </li>
  <li>
    Tap <UI>Claim shift</UI>, or <UI>Pick up permanently</UI>.
  </li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

The card reads **Claiming this shift** until confirmed. Only then is it yours.

A permanent pickup names how many weeks you get and which it skips.

## Rules that apply

- **A permanent pickup takes only what fits.** A week you already work keeps the hours that
  do not clash. A week over your cap is dropped whole, because one tap commits you to
  months. See [Hours, caps, and attribution](/system/hours-rules).
- **A skipped week is not held for you.** A part pickup is final, so each returns in the
  weekly feed.
- **One week over a soft cap is your call.** <UI>Claim anyway</UI> takes it. A hard cap
  refuses, because that limit takes no override.
- **Only Harnwell workers may take Harnwell shifts.** That desk needs training; every other
  house is open to you. See [Core concepts](/system/concepts).
- **<UI>Locked</UI> means gone for good.** A desk that would otherwise sit empty locks two
  hours before its shift. See [How a shift gets covered](/system/coverage-ladder).

## If it goes wrong

**No longer available.** A card marked "2 open" holds two seats; the first two taps take
them.

**Refused for a clash.** You already hold something at that time, at any house.
