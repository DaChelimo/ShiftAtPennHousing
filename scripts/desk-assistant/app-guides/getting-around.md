# The bottom tabs

The app has five tabs along the bottom of the screen. From left to right they are My Shifts, Open, House, Swaps, and More. My Shifts is your personal calendar. Open is the list of open shifts you can pick up. House shows the live desk schedule for your house. Swaps is where swap and hand off requests live. More opens a sheet with everything else.

# What is in the More menu

Tap More (the last tab on the bottom) to open a sheet titled More. It has five rows: Updates, Preferences, Break shifts, Settings, and Assistant. Updates is your notifications feed. Preferences is where you paint the hours you would like to work. Break shifts is the break sign up calendar. Settings has your account, appearance, and sign out. Assistant is the desk question chatbot. When something needs your attention, a dot appears on the More tab.

# Where do I find the Assistant

The Assistant lives under More. Tap the More tab at the bottom, then tap Assistant in the sheet. You can also open it from the Ask button that floats on the main screens. Use it to ask desk questions and how to questions about the app. Its answers are grounded in the official documentation and the current duty schedule.

# Reading your calendar

Open the My Shifts tab to see your schedule. At the top a chip shows your hours for the week you are viewing, for example "This week" with a value like 14h "of 20h soft cap". The label changes to Next week, Last week, or In N weeks as you move between weeks.

# Switching between week and day view

On My Shifts there is a toggle with two options, Week and Day. Week shows the whole week at a glance and is the default. Day shows a single day at a time with a Monday to Sunday strip you can tap to jump between days. If a day has no shifts you see "No shifts this day"; if your house is closed that day you see "House closed".

# Moving between weeks

Use the week navigator at the bottom of My Shifts and Open to move forward or back a week. The title tells you which week you are on: This week, Next week, Last week, or In N weeks. Picking up or dropping a shift in a future week shows up under that week.
