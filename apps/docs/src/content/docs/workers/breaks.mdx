You claim your own break hours on the break calendar. Nobody builds you a break schedule.

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>
    Open <UI>More</UI>, then <UI>Break shifts</UI>.
  </li>
  <li>Pick a day inside the break.</li>
  <li>
    Drag down the empty space to pick your hours.
    <span class="step-note">You pick a time, not a desk. The app fills any open seat.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Tap <UI>Claim</UI>.
  </li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

Your hours fill in with your name, and the <UI>This break</UI> meter climbs.

## Rules that apply

- **The calendar opens 14 days before the break's first day and closes one day before it
  starts.** Every day of the break closes together, whatever day the shift falls on.
- **First come, first served.** A fully staffed block is read only, because a desk can never
  be over-staffed.
- **Thanksgiving, fall break, and spring break cap you at 40 hours.** The cap is hard, so a
  drag past it is refused, not warned. See
  [Hours, caps, and attribution](/system/hours-rules).
- **A shift you give back before the calendar closes returns to the calendar** for anyone
  to take.
- **Tick <UI>I have no hours this break</UI> to stop the reminder three days out.** It does
  not stop you claiming later.

## If it goes wrong

**You only got part of what you dragged.** Somebody claimed the rest first. Drag what is
left.

**The banner reads Claiming closed.** What is unclaimed, and anything given back now, is in
[Open Shifts](/workers/picking-up).

**The drag will not go further.** You are at the break cap, or the next block is full.
