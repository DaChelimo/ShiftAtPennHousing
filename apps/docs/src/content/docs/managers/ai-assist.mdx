The schedule generator drafts a full week from submitted preferences that you edit and publish.

<Callout label="A proposal is a draft, never a publish">
  It writes only to your draft grid. Nothing reaches a worker until you publish.
</Callout>

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>Open the builder on the house and week.</li>
  <li>
    Select <UI>Generate with AI</UI> and leave the tab open.
    <span class="step-note">Days fill into the grid as they are built.</span>
  </li>
  <li>Read the open-seat list first.</li>
  <li>Select <UI>Accept as draft</UI> to keep it, or <UI>Discard</UI> to drop it.</li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

A scored draft and its unfilled seats. Each says whether anyone available could have worked it.

Shifts run at least two hours, on the hour except at desk open and close.

## Rules that apply

- **Hard rules are enforced, not scored.** Harnwell training, headcount, block boundaries,
  hours caps, and availability constrain the generator, so a proposal breaking one never
  reaches you. See [Core concepts](/system/concepts).
- **Coverage beats preference, deliberately.** An empty seat leaves the desk unstaffed, so
  no preference gain can buy one. See
  [How a shift gets covered](/system/coverage-ladder).
- **Generating is gated like building.** An SM generates for their own house; HM, BA, RSM,
  and Administrator for any house. It grants no authority you lack. See
  [Roles and the duty hierarchy](/system/roles).

## If it goes wrong

**Generate is disabled.** No scheduling period covers this week, or the preference deadline is still open, since building starts only when it closes.

**Seats are still open.** Fill them by hand, or publish them as claimable.

**You stopped the run.** <UI>Stop</UI> keeps the days already built. <UI>Stop and clear</UI>
keeps nothing.
