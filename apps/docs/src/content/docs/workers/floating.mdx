A float sends you to another house's desk for part of your shift, then back to your own.

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>
    Open <UI>My Shifts</UI>.
    <span class="step-note">The card sits at the top, and in <UI>Updates</UI>.</span>
  </li>
  <li>Read the house, day, and hours.</li>
  <li>
    Tap <UI>Accept</UI> if you can work it, or <UI>Decline</UI> if you cannot.
  </li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

Until you answer, your week shows that stretch marked <UI>(Pending)</UI>. Accepting clears
the label. Declining reopens the shift at the other house.

## Rules that apply

- **Answer by 10 minutes before the float starts.** The desk needs to know who is walking
  in. See [Floating: overview](/system/floating).
- **Reminders land 6 hours, 2 hours, 1 hour, 30 minutes and 5 minutes before that
  deadline.** You cannot silence them, because only you can answer this one. See
  [Notifications and widgets](/workers/notifications).
- **A float never adds hours.** It moves hours you already had, so no cap is checked. See
  [Hours, caps, and attribution](/system/hours-rules).
- **Declining is remembered.** Your no is your answer for those hours. You are skipped for
  that gap, and for later floats at that house over the same hours. See
  [Floating: the selection rule](/system/floating/deep-dive).

## If it goes wrong

**You said nothing.** Fifteen minutes before the float starts, the app cancels it and tells
your manager. Nobody can commute in that fast, so they pay an Allied guard instead. Decline
early.

**You accepted and now you cannot go.** Call your manager. Nothing automatic pulls you off
once the float is yours, because the other desk is counting on you.
