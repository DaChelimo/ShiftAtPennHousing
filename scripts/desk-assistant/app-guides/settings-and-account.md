# Where do I change app settings

Open More, then tap Settings. At the top is your profile card with your name, house, and role. Below it are four groups: Notifications, Appearance, Hours & limits, and Account.

# Turning notifications on or off

In the Notifications group, "Float assignments", "Shift reminders", and "Schedule published" are always on because they are time critical; you cannot turn them off. Only "General updates" (house-wide broadcasts about open shifts) has a switch you can toggle. Turn it on if you want a notification whenever an open shift is broadcast at your house.

# Switching between light and dark mode

In the Appearance group, tap Theme, then choose System, Light, or Dark from the segmented control. System follows your phone's setting. The change applies immediately and is remembered the next time you open the app.

# Checking your hour caps

The Hours & limits group is read only. It shows your "Weekly soft cap" for the regular period and your "Break-period hard cap" for breaks like winter or spring break. These are the same caps referenced when you claim a shift or set a preference target.

# Replaying an onboarding tour

The Account group has a "Replay app tour" row for the first-run welcome tour, plus a separate replay row for each feature tour: shift management, preferences, break shifts, swaps, the house grid, and open shifts. Tap any of them to jump to that screen and watch the walkthrough again.

# Signing out

Scroll to the bottom of the Account group and tap Sign out. This signs you out of the app on this device; you will need to sign back in to see your schedule again.
