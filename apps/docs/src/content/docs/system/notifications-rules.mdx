Every notification comes from an event below. Only three can be switched off.

## What sends one

<Table>

| Event                                | Who hears it              | Off switch           |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------- | -------------------- |
| A float, and its reminders           | You                       | No                   |
| A swap or handoff, and its outcome   | The other party           | No                   |
| Your shift dropped or cancelled      | You                       | No                   |
| A schedule published                 | Everyone assigned         | No                   |
| Preference windows and break sign-up | Workers                   | No                   |
| A seat opening at your house         | Everyone eligible but you | No                   |
| A seat opening at another house      | Workers eligible there    | Opt in               |
| A seat still uncovered 3 hours out   | Same workers              | On for your house    |
| Your own shift starting soon         | You                       | 2h, 1h, or 30m       |
| Nobody left to float                 | One manager, by ladder    | No                   |

</Table>

Eligible means active student workers, student managers, and housing managers, never a
building manager. Only Harnwell workers hear about a Harnwell seat, because nobody else may
staff that desk. A locked seat is never announced, because nobody can claim it.

## Why it works this way

A notification is optional only when ignoring it costs nothing. A float, a swap, and a
published schedule each need an answer from one person, so none carries a switch. A shift
reminder asks nothing back, so you pick lead times or none. Settings still list the
mandatory channels, on and disabled, so you can see that a swap request always reaches you.

## What this means for you

Never open settings and you get the defaults: an hour's warning before each shift, and your
own house only. Other houses stay silent.

Managers get no push. An Allied request reaches them as a console alert, so silence there
means every desk is accounted for.
