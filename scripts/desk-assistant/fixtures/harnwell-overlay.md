# Perimeter doors

Harnwell perimeter doors stay locked outside posted hours. In summer, perimeter door access
for outside contractors, including water check contractors, is not permitted from the desk.
If a contractor asks you to open a perimeter door, do not grant access. Tell them to check in
through the house office and escalate to the manager on duty.

# Spare key retrieval

Spare keys for Harnwell rooms are held in the front desk key cabinet. Retrieve a spare key
only for a resident verified against the roster, log the key number and the time out, and
require the key be returned the same shift. Never issue a spare key to a guest or an
unverified person.
