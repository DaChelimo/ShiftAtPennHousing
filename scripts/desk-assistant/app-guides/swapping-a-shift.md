# How do I swap a shift

Open the My Shifts tab and tap the shift you want to trade. In the Manage shift sheet tap the card that says Swap it ("Trade with a housemate"). Tap "Choose who to swap with" to open the Propose a swap screen. Pick how much of your shift to give, choose whose shift you want in return, then tap Propose swap. The other person gets a request they can Accept or Decline.

# Choosing how much to give and take

On the Propose a swap screen, the first control asks "How much of your shift to give?" and the timeline shows what you are Giving. Below, "Whose shift do you want?" lets you pick a housemate on that day, and "Hours you want from {name}" sets what you are Taking. The deal card in the middle summarizes it as YOU GIVE on one side and what you get on the other.

# Swapping every week (permanent swap)

On the Propose a swap screen there is a card titled "Make it permanent" with the note "Swap this slot every week for the rest of the period". Turn it on to swap the recurring slot rather than a single week. The deal card then shows a Permanent tag.

# Swapping with more than one person

If no single housemate can take your whole shift, tap "+ Add another person" to split the give across several people. Each person is an independent leg. The confirm button then reads "Propose N swaps" and each recipient responds to their own leg separately.

# Swap versus hand off

At the top of the Propose a swap screen are two pills: Swap and Hand off. Swap means you get hours back in return. Hand off means you give your hours away and get nothing back. Use Hand off when you just need someone to cover and are not looking for a trade. See the separate guide on handing off a shift.
