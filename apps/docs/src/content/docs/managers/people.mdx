The roster is where you add somebody, move them to another house, or take them off the desk.

<Callout label="Two of these empty a desk the moment you confirm">
  Firing vacates every shift the person holds, voids their floats, and deactivates their
  account. A mid-shift gap escalates straight away.

  An immediate transfer releases their future shifts at the old house and voids any live
  float, because from that date they belong somewhere else.

  Tell the person, and the house losing them, before you confirm either one.
</Callout>

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>
    Open <UI>People</UI> and read the house name on the header.
    <span class="step-note">An HM (Housing Manager), BA (Building Administrator) or RSM (Residential Services Manager) can view another house here. Hiring and firing still write to your own.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    To add somebody, select <UI>Hire worker</UI> and enter their name, email and phone.
    <span class="step-note">Their home house is the house you administer. You cannot hire into another one.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Set <UI>Initial role</UI>.
    <span class="step-note">This is the role grant, and it decides everything they can do. See [Which manager are you?](/managers/roles).</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    To move somebody, select <UI>Transfer</UI> on their row, choose <UI>Move to</UI> and
    <UI>When</UI>.
    <span class="step-note">Next season start is the default, or pick a date, or move them immediately.</span>
  </li>
  <li>To take somebody off the roster, select <UI>Fire</UI> on their row and confirm.</li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

The new hire appears active on the roster at once, with hours against this week's cap.

A future transfer changes nothing today. The worker keeps their house, their shifts and
their permissions until the date, and the system applies the move that morning.

## Rules that apply

- **Either side may transfer.** The losing house's HM or BA and the receiving house's HM or
  BA can both do it, so a manager who has agreed to take somebody can finish the move.
- **Hiring and firing stay at your own house**, because a roster is the thing you answer for.
  An SM cannot open this screen at all.
- **Building ahead already uses the new house.** Preference collection and building for an
  upcoming period read the house the worker will belong to then, which is how you pre-build a
  transfer before they arrive.
- **An immediate transfer reopens the old seats.** Their released shifts go back into that
  house's open feed so the vacancy is visible and claimable, not silent.
- **Harnwell needs no special handling.** Somebody transferring out loses their Harnwell
  shifts in the move, since only home-Harnwell workers may staff that desk.

## If it goes wrong

**"You can only fire workers home-housed at your own house."** You are viewing another house
through the switcher. Switch back, or ask that house's HM or BA.

**"No upcoming season was found."** There is no season boundary to default to. Choose a
specific effective date instead.

**"That worker already belongs to the destination house."** Somebody transferred them
already, or the move applied overnight. Refresh the roster before trying again.
