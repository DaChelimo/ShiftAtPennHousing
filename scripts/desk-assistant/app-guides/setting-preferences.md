# How do I set my preferences for next semester

Open More, then tap Preferences. Pick a mode at the top: Available, Preferred, or Cannot. Scroll the day using the time column on the left, then press and drag across the shifts to paint your choice onto the calendar. Preferred means you want that time, Available means you can work it if needed, and Cannot means you must not be scheduled then. Any time you leave unpainted is treated as unavailable when your manager builds the schedule, so paint every block you would take.

# Setting your target weekly hours

At the top of the Preferences screen, the "Target weekly hours" card shows your goal for the period with a soft cap noted underneath, for example "Soft cap 20h this period". Use the plus and minus buttons to raise or lower your target. This is a guideline for your manager when they build the schedule, not a hard limit.

# If you want zero hours this period

Tick "I have no hours this week" on the Target weekly hours card. You will not be scheduled next week while this is on. You can still pick up open shifts later if you change your mind; the toggle just tells the schedule builder to skip you for now. Untick it to set your availability again.

# Saving your preferences

Once you have painted your availability, tap Submit preferences at the bottom. If you come back and change something after submitting, the button reads Save changes instead, and a banner reads "Unsaved changes. Save again before the deadline." Tap Discard to throw away unsaved edits and go back to what you last saved.

# The submission deadline

Preferences has a deadline chip showing when the window closes. You can keep editing and resubmitting right up until the deadline. Once it passes, the screen becomes read only: if you submitted, the banner reads "Submitted. The window has closed. Your manager builds from these"; if you never submitted, it reads "Preferences locked. The submission window has closed." After that, you cannot change your preferences for the period, but you can still pick up open shifts once the schedule is published.
