Every operating date carries exactly one rules profile, which decides how that day behaves.

## What a date carries

All three profiles run the desk 08:00 to 24:00.

<Table>

| Profile             | Weekly cap                          | Schedule             | Floating                      |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Regular school year | 20, soft, overridable               | SM, from preferences | Allowed                       |
| Short break         | 40 hard, or 20 soft at spring fling | Claimed, first come  | Allowed                       |
| Winter break        | 40, hard                            | Claimed, first come  | Off. Only Harnwell stays open |

</Table>

A break date inside a semester takes the break profile, settled in advance. An unassigned
date, or a summer date with no open house, is dormant: no blocks and no notifications.

## Summer is authored, not seeded

Summer moves too much to seed. The Administrator authors it as a season, and each date one
of its settings changes becomes an ordinary profile.

## When a desk shrinks

Changes reach future shifts only, because past blocks are history. If a future desk holds
more workers than its new headcount, the excess are cancelled, not grandfathered.

made the change.

## Why it works this way

One profile per date means no date has two answers, summer included.

Read a date's profile and you can predict its cap and floating.
