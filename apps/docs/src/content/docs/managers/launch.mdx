Launching a house makes it visible to its workers and starts the coverage chain on its
desk.

<Callout label="A live house pages real people">
  The escalation chain skips a pre-launch desk, because a desk nobody has opened has
  nobody to page. Go live and every vacant block starts broadcasting, floating, and paging
  Allied.
</Callout>

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>Sign in as the Administrator.</li>
  <li>Open <UI>Launch</UI> and turn on <UI>Staggered launch</UI>.</li>
  <li>Confirm the house has a published schedule.</li>
  <li>Set that house's toggle to <UI>Live</UI>.</li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

The house's tag flips to <UI>Live</UI>, and its workers get their week instead of the
not-yet-on-the-system screen. Launching creates and changes no shift.

## Rules that apply

- **Only the Administrator can change launch state**, or the master switch behind it. No
  reason for that limit is recorded anywhere, so treat it as fixed. See
  [Which manager are you?](/managers/roles).
- **A new house starts pre-launch**, so a house you are setting up stays dark.
- **Managers are never gated.** Build and publish a pre-launch house before any worker
  sees it. See [Publishing a schedule](/managers/publishing).
- **Automated floating needs a second live house.** With one, the float step finds nobody
  and is skipped, while broadcast and the Allied page still run. See
  [Floating](/system/floating).

## If it goes wrong

**Every house reads live and the toggles do nothing.** The master switch is off by default,
and it overrides every house toggle.

**The console refuses you.** You are not the Administrator. Ask them.

**A house went live too early.** Set it back to pre launch: workers go dark, escalation
stops, and the first launch date is kept.
