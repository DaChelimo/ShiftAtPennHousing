These sixteen terms carry a precise meaning you cannot guess from the sentence around
them. Every other term on this site is defined where it is used.

## Where work happens

<Table>

| Term               | What it means                                                                                           |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **House**          | One of Penn's 13 college houses. You are hired into one. See [Core concepts](/system/concepts).          |
| **Desk**           | The post a house staffs. Yours differs from your house while you are floated.                            |
| **Block**          | 30 minutes, on the hour or the half hour. Nothing moves in less.                                         |
| **Recurring slot** | A house, weekday, and time band you own every week of the period.                                        |

</Table>

## Giving up and trading shifts

<Table>

| Term               | What it means                                                                                           |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Drop**           | Giving up one occurrence. It goes to the open feed, and you claim it back like anyone else. See [Dropping a shift](/workers/dropping). |
| **Permanent drop** | Giving up every remaining occurrence of a recurring slot. Never breaks, which have no recurrence. See [Dropping every week](/workers/dropping-permanently). |
| **Swap**           | Two workers exchanging spans. Nothing moves until the other accepts. See [How a swap resolves](/system/swaps-explained). |
| **Handoff**        | A one-way give or take-over. Hours move one way, and no cap is checked.                                  |

</Table>

## When a desk is short

<Table>

| Term                  | What it means                                                                                        |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Coverage floor**    | One worker present. Coverage is chased only for a desk that would otherwise be empty. See [How a shift gets covered](/system/coverage-ladder). |
| **Coverage lock**     | Two hours out, an empty desk's seats stop being claimable. The lock never lifts.                      |
| **Allied**            | Paid outside cover. A manager places the call, because the system cannot buy it.                      |
| **Float**             | Part of your shift moves to another desk, then you return. It relocates hours, never adds them. See [Floating](/system/floating). |
| **Source house**      | The house lending the floater. Lending never leaves it below one worker.                              |
| **Destination house** | The house receiving them. Harnwell lends but never receives, because its desk needs its own training. |

</Table>

## Hours

<Table>

| Term         | What it means                                                                                |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Soft cap** | A weekly hours ceiling that warns you and lets you proceed. See [Hours and caps](/system/hours-rules). |
| **Hard cap** | A weekly hours ceiling that refuses. Nobody overrides it.                                    |

</Table>
