## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>
    Open <UI>House</UI>.
    <span class="step-note">It opens on your house, this week.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Tap the house name to read another house.
    <span class="step-note">The desk phone sits under the name.</span>
  </li>
  <li>Use the week bar to change weeks.</li>
  <li>Tap a block to see who holds it.</li>
  <li>
    Call or email them, or tap <UI>Call the desk</UI>.
  </li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

The card gives their phone, email, and home house, which can differ from the desk they
staff.

## Rules that apply

- **The list holds live houses only.** A house that has not launched has no schedule.
  See [Core concepts](/system/concepts).
- **Every house is readable, none editable**, because you hold no write power. See
  [Core concepts](/system/concepts).
- **Green means a non-home worker**, floated in or claimed, so the desk is not theirs. See
  [When you get floated](/workers/floating).
- **A block with nobody's name is an open seat**, still there to be claimed. See
  [Picking up open shifts](/workers/picking-up).
- **"(Pending)" means the float is unanswered**, so treat that desk as unsettled. See
  [How a shift gets covered](/system/coverage-ladder).
- **A swap mark means nothing has moved.** The desk stays as shown until the other person
  accepts. See [How a swap actually resolves](/system/swaps-explained).
- **The grid is the record**, updated the moment anything changes.

## If it goes wrong

**The house reads Closed.** It is shut on those dates: no shifts, no open seats.

**Nobody is on the block.** Cover is chased only when a desk would be empty, so this
seat waits in the open feed.

**No phone on the card.** No number is saved, so email them or call the desk.
