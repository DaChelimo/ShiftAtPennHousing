A break takes its dates out of the built schedule and opens them for workers to claim.

<Callout label="Only an Administrator declares a break">
  SM, HM, BA and RSM cannot, because one break rewrites every house's calendar at once.
</Callout>

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>
    Enter the name, the <UI>Type</UI>, and the start and end dates.
    <span class="step-note">Type sets the hours cap: 40 hard for Thanksgiving, fall break and spring break, 20 soft for spring fling. Hard refuses a claim past the cap; soft only warns. See [Hours, caps, and attribution](/system/hours-rules).</span>
  </li>
  <li>Set each house: open or closed, <UI>Staff</UI>, and its weekday and weekend hours.</li>
  <li>
    Select <UI>Preview changes</UI>.
    <span class="step-note">It lists shifts created, seats voided, and every worker who loses one.</span>
  </li>
  <li>Select <UI>Declare break</UI>, or <UI>Save break</UI> on one that exists.</li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

The break appears under <UI>Declared breaks</UI>. <UI>Remove</UI> returns its dates to the
regular school year and rebuilds the shifts.

## Rules that apply

- **A break may only cover regular school year dates,** so nothing already authored is
  overwritten. See [The operating calendar](/system/calendar).
- **Floating is a switch on the break.** With it off, an uncovered desk goes from broadcast
  straight to Allied. See [How a shift gets covered](/system/coverage-ladder).

## If it goes wrong

**The range overlaps a season or another break.** Shrink that one first.

**Preview lists workers losing shifts.** Closing a desk, or cutting its staff, cancels their
future shifts. The cancellation goes out when you save, so tell them first.

**You declared it inside 14 days.** The window runs from the break's first day, so workers
get what is left of it.
