The app pings you when a shift needs you, and a widget shows the same thing without opening
it.

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>
    On <UI>My Shifts</UI>, tap <UI>Turn on</UI> in the alerts row above your schedule.
    <span class="step-note">
      The row stays there until alerts are on. Ignoring it costs you nothing.
    </span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Open <UI>More</UI>, then <UI>Settings</UI>.
  </li>
  <li>
    Under <UI>Shift reminders</UI>, tick the lead times you want.
    <span class="step-note">2 hours, 1 hour, 30 minutes, in any mix. You start on 1 hour.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Turn on <UI>Open shifts at other houses</UI> to hear about extra work elsewhere.
    <span class="step-note">It starts off, because another house is not your coverage.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Add a widget from your phone's home screen: <UI>Upcoming shifts</UI> or{' '}
    <UI>Open shifts</UI>.
  </li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

Settings lists the channels you cannot turn off too, switched on and greyed, so you can see
that a swap request will always reach you.

Ticking nothing under <UI>Shift reminders</UI> reads **Off**, not blank. That is a real
choice, not a broken screen.

## Rules that apply

- **Anything that needs your answer cannot be silenced.** A float, a swap request, break
  sign-up opening, a preference deadline, a published schedule: someone is waiting on you.
  See [What triggers a notification](/system/notifications-rules).
- **A float chases you five times.** Reminders land 6 hours, 2 hours, 1 hour, 30 minutes and
  5 minutes before your deadline, because only you can answer it. See
  [When you get floated](/workers/floating).
- **A drop at your own house always pings you.** Your desk's coverage is not optional
  reading, so that one has no off switch. See [Picking up open shifts](/workers/picking-up).
- **A reminder is checked before it fires.** Give the shift away and its reminder is dropped,
  so you are never nudged toward a shift that is not yours.
- **Widgets only look.** Tapping one opens the app, because nothing is claimed or
  acknowledged from a home screen. What it shows is a snapshot and can lag a little.

## If it goes wrong

**You tapped <UI>Not now</UI> and want alerts back.** Your phone never asked you, so turn
notifications on for Shift in your phone's own settings.

**Nothing has pinged you in days.** Check that Shift may send you notifications. A push that
keeps failing is retried for about seven hours and then given up on, and your manager is
shown the ones that failed.

**The widget is behind.** Open the app and it catches up. Trust the app, not the widget.
