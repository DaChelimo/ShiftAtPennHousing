A swap trades hours with one housemate. Nothing moves until they say yes.

## Do it

<Steps>
  <li>
    Open <UI>My Shifts</UI> and tap the shift.
  </li>
  <li>
    Choose <UI>Swap it</UI>, then <UI>Choose who to swap with</UI>.
  </li>
  <li>
    Pick <UI>Swap</UI> or <UI>Hand off</UI>.
    <span class="step-note">A hand off is one way: they get the hours, you get nothing back.</span>
  </li>
  <li>Pick the housemate.</li>
  <li>
    Drag the sliders to set your hours and theirs.
    <span class="step-note">Part of a shift is fine.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    Tap <UI>Propose swap</UI>.
  </li>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

Your card reads **Swap pending**. Theirs shows the request at once, no refresh needed.
When they accept, both calendars change together.

## Rules that apply

- **A pending swap freezes the shift.** One block can sit in only one request, so you
  cannot drop or re-swap it. See [How a swap resolves](/system/swaps-explained).
- **A swap dies 3 hours before the earlier shift starts.** Nobody is left arranging cover
  at the last minute.
- **A hand off can be recorded late.** These are agreed at the desk, so you get 24 hours
  after the shift ends to log who worked it.
- **Both of you must be allowed at the other desk.** Harnwell needs its own training, and
  the check runs again on acceptance. See [How floating works](/system/floating).
- **Hours you take count against your week**, so your cap can block a swap. A hand off
  skips that check. See [Hours, caps, and attribution](/system/hours-rules).

## If it goes wrong

**It disappeared.** Your shift was dropped or floated before they answered, so the request
was voided. You are both told.

**Nobody answered.** It expired, nothing changed.

**They declined.** The shift is yours again, and unfrozen.
