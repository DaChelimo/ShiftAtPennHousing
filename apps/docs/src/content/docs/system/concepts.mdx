## How it works

Assigning, dropping, claiming, floating, swapping, and counting hours all move in whole
blocks, never part of one, so an hour is always two blocks.

A block holds as many seats as the desk needs.

<Table>

| House               | Seats per block (school year) |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Harnwell            | 2, 08:00 to 24:00             |
| Upper Quad          | 3, 08:00 to 24:00             |
| The other 11 houses | 1, 08:00 to 24:00             |

</Table>

Each seat has one owner, so two racing workers cannot both win and the loser is told so.
Nobody holds two seats of one block.

A house is where a worker is hired. A desk is the post being covered. They split on a
float: an Upper Quad worker covering Lauder staffs Lauder's desk and keeps Upper Quad.

All times are Penn local, America/New_York. A block is always 30 real minutes, even on
clock-change nights, so a clock change moves no hours.

## Why it works this way

One fixed unit keeps every operation exact and comparable. Seat counts belong to the desk,
not the house, because a summer season can change one partway through.

## What this means for you

Blocks bound what you can drag, seats start [the coverage ladder](/system/coverage-ladder),
and desk against house drives [Floating](/system/floating).
