## 3. Description of the service

Shift@PennHousing is an internal staff-scheduling and desk-coverage tool. It provides, among other things: viewing, claiming, dropping, and swapping shifts; automated coverage escalation (broadcast, float assignment, and, where enabled, contracted Allied coverage); submitting scheduling preferences and leave requests; receiving push notifications about shifts and required actions; manager tools for building, publishing, and administering schedules, including AI-assisted schedule generation; and a knowledge-base and Desk Assistant reference tool for on-desk procedures.

The App reflects real-world staffing decisions. Actions taken in the App, such as claiming a shift, acknowledging a float, or submitting a permanent transfer, have real operational consequences and may affect your actual work obligations and hours.
